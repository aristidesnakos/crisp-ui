## Patterns

- [Status and notify](/docs/components/status-notify): a status headline, a confirm-then-send action, and one roster of who hears about it.
- [Approval step](/docs/components/approval-step) (Act stage): a sign-off with a stated reason and meaning. Comes after a data table, leads to [Confirm send](/docs/components/confirm-send) and then an audit timeline.

- [Audit timeline](/docs/components/audit-timeline): the Record stage. A read-only "who did what, when, and why" list for the events your server has recorded. It comes after confirm-send and closes the loop back to the status strip.

## Parts

Each part works alone. The pattern above is built from all five.

- [Status strip](/docs/components/status-strip): a headline number, a segmented bar and a legend, with a slot for the action.
- [Recipient roster](/docs/components/recipient-roster): one list of people with a switch per channel.
- [Confirm send](/docs/components/confirm-send): click, confirm with the count, send, "Sent to N".
- [Save bar](/docs/components/save-bar): "Unsaved changes" with Discard and Save, hidden when clean.
- [Notify envelope](/docs/components/notify-envelope): pure helpers for rosters, list comparison and the one-message To/Cc envelope.
